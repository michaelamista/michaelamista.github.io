Welcome

Hi, I'm Michael!

I'm 23 years old, Italian, and deeply driven by curiosity, positivity, and a continuous desire to grow. Whether it's diving into a new technology or questioning how something works under the hood, I’m the type of person who constantly asks, "How can this be improved?"

That mindset is what led me to the world of computer science—an ever-evolving field full of challenges, new ideas, and endless learning. I love thinking outside the box and analyzing the reasoning behind implementations, not just how they work, but why they work the way they do.

My core interests revolve around wireless networks, IoT, mobile development, and making technology more usable and meaningful. I believe that great tech should not only work well but feel effortless to use.

Outside the tech space, I’m always open to new experiences and perspectives. I find inspiration in simplicity, problem-solving, and in people who aren’t afraid to rethink what’s considered “normal.”

If I had to sum myself up in a few words: curious, analytical, and enthusiastically adaptable.
